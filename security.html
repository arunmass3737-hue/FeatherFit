1. Payment Card Security Architecture

Feather Fits operates under strict compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS SAQ A). When you purchase apparel on our store:

  • Zero Plaintext Storage: Feather Fits never stores your full 16-digit debit/credit card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV/CVC codes on our databases or web servers.
  • Direct-to-Gateway Tokenization: Checkout card details are ingested via iframes hosted directly by regulated payment gateways (Stripe / Razorpay / Cashfree) and tokenized in real time.
  • 3D Secure 2.0 (3DS2): All electronic card payments are verified with your issuing bank via mandatory OTP or biometrics.

2. Doorstep UPI & Cash on Delivery (COD) Safeguards

For customers selecting Doorstep Payment / COD:

  • Orders are validated through phone number SMS OTP verification to stop spam and bot submissions.
  • Delivery personnel provide an authorized, tamper-evident dynamic QR code at the time of parcel handover for seamless UPI scan-and-pay.
  • Cash collections are backed by immediate digital SMS receipt generation upon receipt.

3. Network & Infrastructure Defense

Our e-commerce infrastructure features defense-in-depth engineering:

  • Cloudflare / Enterprise WAF: Comprehensive Web Application Firewall with real-time DDoS mitigation, rate-limiting, and bad-bot blocking.
  • Automated Vulnerability Audits: Continuous vulnerability scans and dependencies updates to eliminate zero-day risks.
  • Strict CSP & Security Headers: Strict Content-Security-Policy (CSP), HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, and X-Frame-Options headers applied to all responses.

4. Reporting Vulnerabilities (Bug Bounty & Disclosure)

We welcome security researchers to inspect our public endpoints responsibly. If you discover an exploit or vulnerability, please contact our security team immediately at:

security@featherfits.com

We commit to acknowledging valid security reports within 24 hours and keeping you updated as remediation is deployed.
