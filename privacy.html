Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection Act

1. Information We Collect

When you browse Feather Fits or place an order for our premium streetwear, we collect necessary personal details to fulfill your delivery, including your name, delivery address, phone number, and email. We do not store raw credit card numbers or banking PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To dispatch shipments via verified express logistics partners (Blue Dart Express & Delhivery).
  • To provide live SMS/WhatsApp order tracking updates and delivery OTP verification.
  • To manage your personalized fit recommendations and saved delivery addresses.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your connection to Feather Fits is encrypted end-to-end using 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL). Payment data is tokenized securely via certified PCI-DSS SAQ A compliant payment gateways.

4. Contact Privacy Grievance Officer

If you have any questions or wish to delete your account data, contact our Data Grievance Officer at privacy@featherfits.com or call our Chennai customer support desk at +91 98765 43210.
